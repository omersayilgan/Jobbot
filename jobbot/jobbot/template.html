How the score works. Points come from four signals: the role's title family, which of your CV skill clusters the posting asks for, seniority fit (entry-level up, "Principal" and "10+ years" down), and your transcript — modules you scored well in (ROS 1.0, Flight Control Lab 1.7, C++ 1.7, Advanced & Nonlinear Control 2.0, Real-Time Systems 2.0) push a role up, while thermo-fluids, propulsion, structures and RF/analog subjects push it down. Postings demanding C1 German are discounted rather than hidden.

Regenerate this page after a fresh pull with python3 run.py fetch && python3 run.py report. Build a ready-to-send application pack for any role with python3 run.py pack <uid>.

Sources: Greenhouse, Ashby, Personio, Lever and Recruitee public job-board APIs. Employers hiring only through Workday or SAP SuccessFactors (Airbus, MTU, Siemens, Rohde & Schwarz) are not covered here — check those career pages directly.
